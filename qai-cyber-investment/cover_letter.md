Please find enclosed our manuscript, "Quantum-Inspired Artificial Intelligence for Cybersecurity Investment Prioritization: A Hybrid Decision Intelligence Framework," which we submit for consideration as a **Short Communication** in *Knowledge-Based Systems*.

Deciding how to allocate a finite security budget across competing controls is a high-stakes managerial problem that current practice addresses with single-criterion rankings that treat each control in isolation. This ignores defence-in-depth interactions and systematically misallocates capital. Our work reframes the problem and shows how to solve it.

The manuscript falls squarely within the journal's scope, contributing to its leading topics of **intelligent decision support systems**, **data-driven optimization**, **knowledge representation and engineering**, and **computational-intelligence systems**. Specifically, we offer:

1. A new **problem formulation** — cybersecurity budgeting as a quadratic, synergy-aware knapsack that explicitly models defence-in-depth complementarities.
2. A new **knowledge-based architecture** — a three-layer pipeline uniting data-driven prediction, fuzzy expert-rule correction, and a quantum-inspired evolutionary optimizer, producing transparent and auditable recommendations.
3. A reproducible **empirical finding** — across twenty independent seeds the framework reaches a 0.06% mean gap to the best solution, and we show that modelling control synergies, rather than the choice of optimizer, is the decisive factor: an exact optimizer of the conventional additive objective is outperformed by interaction-aware heuristics.

The complete implementation is dependency-free (Python standard library only) and released openly to support full reproducibility, including the generation of all figures and tables.

We confirm that this manuscript is original, has not been published previously, and is not under consideration elsewhere. All authors have approved the submission and declare no competing interests. The use of a generative AI assistant during preparation is disclosed in the manuscript in accordance with the journal's policy; the authors reviewed and edited all content and take full responsibility for it.

Thank you for considering our submission. We look forward to the reviewers' feedback.
